What is g/km?

This is the Carbon Cost of your car. It measures how many grams of CO2 come out of the tailpipe for every single kilometre you drive.

Lower is better. A typical 4x4 (200g+) creates twice the impact of a small hybrid (~100g).

Aviation & Long Distance Travel

How is air travel measured?

Flight impact is measured in kg of CO2 per passenger. Short flights have higher intensity due to takeoff energy, while long-haul flights accumulate massive totals due to distance.

Carbon ROI: A single flight to London emits more than a year of driving a small car.
