Tailpipe Carbon Rankings (Australia)

What is g/km?

This is the Carbon Cost of your car. It measures how many grams of CO2 come out of the tailpipe for every single kilometre you drive.

Lower is better. While EVs emit 0g at the tailpipe, a typical luxury V8 or heavy utility vehicle can exceed 300g per kilometre.
